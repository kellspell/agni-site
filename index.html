Why Agni

Everything hard about local AI, handled for you.

Running AI on your own machine usually means command lines, drivers and guesswork. Agni turns it into something that feels like an app store.

Private by design

Models run on your computer. Your questions, documents and answers never leave it, so it works for personal and confidential material.

Checked against your hardware

Agni scans your computer and marks every model green, amber or red before you download, so you never wait for gigabytes that won't run.

One click to install

Pick a model, press Install, watch the progress bar. Agni downloads it, checks it and sets it up. No terminal, Python or drivers to manage.

License-checked

Every model in the store shows its license in plain words, so you know whether you can use it for work before you rely on it.

Free on your computer

Running models on your own machine is free, for good, with no account needed. You only pay if you want the extra connectors in Agni Plus.

Your choice of chat app

Chat inside Agni, or install a different chat window, such as Hollama or AnythingLLM, from the Apps & Extensions tab. They all use the models you installed.

How it works

From download to first answer in minutes.

  1. Open Agni

    Agni checks your computer's memory, graphics card and free space, and sets up the engine that runs models.

  2. Pick a green model

    Browse by what you want to do: chat, writing, coding. Green means it runs great on your machine. Press Install.

  3. Start chatting

    Ask anything, right away, with nothing sent over the internet. Remove a model in one click when you're done with it.

The hardware check

Know before you download.

AI models range from 1 GB to over 100 GB, and the wrong one can freeze an ordinary laptop. Agni compares what each model needs with what your computer has, and tells you in plain words.

Runs great

Fits comfortably in your memory or graphics card. Replies come quickly.

e.g. a 3B model on 16 GB
Runs, but slower

It works, with replies taking a little longer. Fine for occasional use.

e.g. a 7B model, no graphics card
Won't run well

Needs more memory or disk space than you have. Agni won't let you download it by mistake.

e.g. a 70B model on 16 GB

Questions

Good to know.

Is Agni free?

Yes. Browsing the store, installing models, chatting, creating images and the basic connectors are free, for good, with no account needed. Agni Plus is an optional subscription that adds more connectors.

What do I get with Agni Plus?

Every connector, including Documents, Memory and Git, plus each new connector and automation as it's added. Your models and chats work the same either way; Plus gives them more tools.

How do I upgrade, or cancel?

In Agni, click Sign in at the top and sign in with a code sent to your email. Choose a plan, and pay on Stripe's secure checkout page. To change your card or cancel, open Manage subscription in the same place. If you cancel, Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Do I need a powerful computer or a graphics card?

No. Small models run well on an ordinary laptop with 8 GB of memory. A graphics card or an Apple silicon Mac makes bigger models faster, and Agni's hardware check shows you exactly what your machine can handle.

Does anything I type get sent to the internet?

No. Once a model is installed, your conversations happen entirely on your computer. Agni only goes online to download models and connectors, to update its store, and, if you sign in, to check your plan. The Web pages connector is the exception you choose: when you ask about a page, it fetches that page.

Where do the models come from?

From Hugging Face and ModelScope, where AI labs such as Alibaba, Meta, Microsoft and Mistral publish them. Agni downloads them directly from there and verifies every file.

Can I use these models for my business?

Many of them, yes. Each model in the store shows its license and whether commercial use is allowed, allowed with conditions, or not allowed, so you can choose with confidence.

Which languages does Agni speak?

The app is in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Arabic and Hindi, and picks your computer's language automatically. The AI models themselves reply in whatever language you write in.
